Steve (2026-07-06): "Toss a Bead ID as if it's common knowledge and accessible to all... relax and take a breath. slow down and lay out your points." He could tell there was substance but couldn't extract it because of terseness ("tercity") and presumed knowledge.

**Why:** Steve doesn't hold the bead graph in his head. A naked ID (st-d5f, co-59ky) is a pointer he can't dereference. Terse-with-jargon reads as noise; terse-with-plain-language reads as signal.

**How to apply:** First mention of any bead = ID + title + one-line what-it-is. Prefer the thing's name over its ID in running prose. When laying out multi-point arguments, structure each point as claim → why it matters → proposal, in complete sentences. Cross-zgent arguments should be written as self-contained A2A memos COO can consume without this session's context. Related: [[probabilistic-not-absolutist]].
