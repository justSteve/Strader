Steve's role ruling, 2026-07-27, in his words: he must be "the final call on all
aspects of a given trade, sizing included — I am the responsible party," but he is
NOT "an arbiter of what is the objective 'correctness' of PA" and is not ready to
decide "that a given element of PA is 'confirmation'." Authoritative confirmation
belongs to hindsight — the replay record and the acuity sweep harness.

**Why:** partly his place on the learning curve, which improves; mostly a permanent
bandwidth fact, in his own framing — "the scale and pace of PA is such that human
scale will never match agents." This extends [[trading-since-2021]]: never position
Steve's judgment as the validation instrument, now including real-time grading of
recognizer output. The original st-n62 LEG A design was retired for exactly this.

**How to apply:**

- Never build a workflow where Steve labels PA correctness — real/marginal/noise
  grading, confirmation arbitration. Hindsight measurement grades; his chair time
  is for HIS learning (drills, replays), not for producing labels.
- Agent attention on live PA is duty-cycled, not continuous: deterministic code
  watches ~90% of the session and an interactive session switches on at pivotal
  moments — the "x-ray machine" frame, design in st-vqa.
- Live agent reads are graded by the same hindsight harness as the recognizer. No
  participant is above the tape.
