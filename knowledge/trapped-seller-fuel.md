# Trapped-Seller Fuel

On 2026-08-19, reading Carmine's 7738/39 reclaim against our tape, the read
that explained his conviction was: the entry is powerful in proportion to
**how much selling has accumulated at and under the level through prior price
action** — sellers who are underwater the moment the level reclaims, whose
covering is the fuel for the move. "The break below fails, the trapped
sellers are the fuel." Steve asked for that insight to be a feature we
automatically look for when considering entries. This file defines the
feature. Long side written out; the short side mirrors.

## The epistemic rule that governs the feature

From the same session, on challenge from Steve ("accurate reading of trapped
sellers or the very common certitude you are trained to exhibit?"), the
mechanism story was re-graded: **trades tell us where aggression happened,
not who is still holding or where anyone's stop sits.** The two-cohort,
stops-at-42-44 story was narrative — consistent with the tape and equally
consistent with other explanations. The structural read (lid, absorption,
history, thinness) was arithmetic over our tape and stands.

So the feature measures and reports **only what the tape shows**, in Steve's
units. Its ceiling claim is "consistent with trapped sellers." It never
narrates cohorts, stop locations, or who is covering. A surface that renders
this feature renders numbers, not a story.

## The five measured components

For a candidate entry level **L** (a Mancini level, a reclaim/continuation
trigger, an FBD low), evaluated at consideration time:

1. **History** — how long price traded capped under L before the current
   approach: hours/sessions from the corpus (15-min bars, prior 1–3
   sessions), plus whether the letter names it. 08-19: sellers had leaned on
   39 for a day and a half, not since 08:55.
2. **Underwater aggression** — the count: total hit-bid (sell-aggressor)
   volume printed in [L − 3, L + 1] during the current lid window. That
   volume sold into the level recently; the moment price holds above L it is
   underwater. This is the measurable "number of trapped sellers." 08-19:
   the −582 print at 7739.00 after the V, and the lid-window sell aggression
   above the higher lows.
3. **Lid rejections** — bar-highs within ~2 pts under L over the last 30–40
   min, flat or stepping down. 08-19: five rejections 7738–41 in 25 minutes.
4. **Absorbed dips** — higher lows with positive bar delta while price makes
   no upward progress (buyers aggressive at the dips, price not breaking
   down). 08-19: lows 7720.75 → 7726.5 → 7728.75 → 7731.5 with +666/+391/+125.
5. **Thin above** — volume-by-price from L to the first shelf holding real
   volume; the shelf is the first target, the gap is the run room. 08-19:
   7742–46 held a quarter of 7739's volume, nothing above 7746 in RTH, first
   shelf 7747–50 — exactly Carmine's stated target and exit.

Components 1, 2, 3 and 5 are pure arithmetic over the 2,000-lot bars and
volume-by-price the bridge already builds. 4 is the same read
[[reclaim-under-the-lid]] condition 5 makes, mechanized as
higher-lows-with-positive-delta.

## How it surfaces

At entry consideration — price within a few points of a candidate level on
the live footprint page, or a level named in a drill/coach exchange — the
feature renders as one fuel line, all five components, Steve's units:

> **Fuel at 7739:** leaned on 1.5 sessions · 5 lid rejections in 25 min ·
> 9.4K sold into the lid, underwater above 40 · dips bought (+1.8K on higher
> lows) · thin to 7747 (¼ volume)

A component that is absent renders as absent ("no lid yet", "heavy above") —
the missing half of the picture is the read as much as the present half.
High fuel is not a signal to enter; it is one input Steve weighs. The feature
never fires advice.

## Validation

Predictive value is a corpus measurement, not an impression: across replay
days, does high fuel at a reclaim/continuation trigger predict follow-through
past L (the postmortem's for/against windows), against the base rate at the
same trigger types with low fuel? The postmortem ledger
(`data/measurement/postmortem/`) is the instrument. Until that measurement
exists, the feature is a *display* of what the tape shows, not a graded
signal.
