All user-facing language (coach captions, drill UI, docs) says **stages**: flush → stall → flip → confirm. The stage names themselves are fine. Code identifiers (anatomy_beat event, beats field) unchanged — display text only.

Same session: "tighter leash" on drill material — his retention doesn't demand non-repetition; drills use a FIXED reference deck (docs/drills/scenario-catalog.md, the pro forma collection) and repeat it until pass bars are met. Fresh days join the deck deliberately, not automatically.

**Why:** Metaphor collisions cost him real cognition mid-drill; novelty-for-its-own-sake wastes the retention he does have.

**How to apply:** Vocabulary check before coaching; build lesson content around the catalog's scenarios (S1–S6, F1–F4, T1–T3), not around whatever day is newest. Related: [[spell-out-bead-references]], [[grow-into-live]].
