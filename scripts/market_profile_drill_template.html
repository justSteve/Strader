What am I looking at?

A Market Profile (TPO) for one session, building half-hour bracket by half-hour: A (8:30–9:00 CT), B, C, and so on. Each bracket's letter is stamped on every price row it traded — the row with the most letters is the Point of Control (POC), the fairest price of the day. The band holding ~70% of the letters around the POC is the Value Area (VAH high edge, VAL low edge). The high/low of brackets A+B is the Initial Balance (IB) — drawn automatically once bracket B closes, teal dashed lines. A row touched by exactly one letter, away from the day's extremes, is a single print — a fast, rejected move the market tends to come back and repair.

Phases

  • Watch — no quiz. Developing POC / Value Area / singles render live after every bracket so you can see the read evolve. Run this first on a new day.
  • Calls — overlays hidden. Playback pauses at each checkpoint bracket and runs its quiz: a day-type call, a click-the-POC call, and (from bracket F on) a single-print flag pass. The first checkpoint also asks about the Initial Balance. Day-type calls before the close are logged as early reads and judged only at the final checkpoint — that's where you see your own call history and find out when you locked onto the right shape.

Day types (doc 03)

  • D-shape — balance / rotation. Two-sided trade, a bell around one value area. The friendly day for the late-day butterfly.
  • P-shape / b-shape — one-sided. A thin stem (fast, early move) under or over a bulge where the day built acceptance. P = stem low, bulge high (short-covering rally). b = stem high, bulge low (long liquidation).
  • Trend day — elongated, no bulge. The auction never finds two-sided business. No rotation, no pin — the day that hurts the fly.

Close of day

After the last bracket: the truth day-type + why, your call history, the full overlay, and a volume twin toggle — per-row session volume as faint bars behind the letters, with the time-POC and volume-POC marked separately. Time is not volume; sometimes they agree, sometimes they don't.
