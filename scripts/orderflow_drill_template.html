What am I looking at?

This is one full trading day replayed as volume bars: every column is exactly the same number of contracts changing hands (shown in the header), not the same amount of time. When columns arrive fast, the market is busy; when they crawl, it's quiet. The strip below the chart shows each bar's real duration.

Reading one cell (a price inside a bar)

  • Blue = buyers were the aggressors at that price (they crossed the spread to lift sellers' offers). Red = sellers were the aggressors (they hit buyers' bids).
  • Stronger color = more contracts traded at that price in this bar.
  • The number is the cell's net delta (buy-aggressor minus sell-aggressor volume). Hover any cell for the full breakdown. Switch the Cells menu to see totals or the raw bid×ask pair.
  • The outlined cell is the bar's POC — the price where the most volume traded.
  • A dashed empty cell is real: price jumped across that level without a single trade (a micro low-volume node).

What to look FOR (the force-and-effect compass)

  • Force with effect — heavy one-sided color and price moving that way: a healthy move, expect continuation.
  • Force without effect — heavy aggressive volume but price stops: someone big is absorbing. Watch for reversal.
  • Effect without force — price makes a new high/low but the session Δ readout doesn't follow: a hollow move.
  • Footer of each column = the bar's total delta. The Session Δ readout is the running sum — its trend matters, not its level.

How to run a rep

  1. Click a level chip (session levels, or M: chips from Mancini's letter). You'll be dropped ~10 bars before price first visited it.
  2. Press Space and watch the approach: is the aggression toward the level being rewarded (bars keep moving) or eaten (heavy color, no progress)?
  3. When price gets within a point, the replay pauses and asks: reject or accept. Answer, watch the tape decide, and your running score builds below.
  4. Use the visit buttons above the chart to replay other approaches to the same level.
