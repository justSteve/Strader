What am I looking at?

This is one full trading day replayed as volume bars: every column is exactly the same number of contracts changing hands (shown in the header), not the same amount of time. When columns arrive fast, the market is busy; when they crawl, it's quiet. Each column's footer carries the bar's real duration as its colored fill height — tall fill = slow bar, short fill = urgent tape. That height is on a fixed absolute scale — full = 120s, the same on every day you drill, so heights are comparable across days and you can learn what a given pace looks like.

Reading one cell (a price inside a bar)

  • Blue = buyers were the aggressors at that price (they crossed the spread to lift sellers' offers). Red = sellers were the aggressors (they hit buyers' bids).
  • Stronger color = more contracts traded at that price in this bar.
  • The number is the cell's net delta (buy-aggressor minus sell-aggressor volume). Hover any cell for the full breakdown. Switch the Cells menu to see totals or the raw bid×ask pair.
  • The outlined cell is the bar's POC — the price where the most volume traded.
  • A dashed empty cell is real: price jumped across that level without a single trade (a micro low-volume node).
  • A cell that flashes amber then keeps a thin edge rule and a corner glyph is one the stack spoke about: left rule ▮ = imbalance stack price, right rule ◢ = swept price, violet top rule ◆ = the pivot a divergence measured against, green bottom rule ▬ = a setup's anchor. Hover names it; click opens what the stack said. Press x to hide or show all cues.
  • The panel left of the columns is the volume profile anchored at the prior day's 08:30 CT open — one row per price on the same rows as the cells; red grows from the left for sell-aggressor volume, blue stacks after it for buy-aggressor; faint = everything before today's 08:30 open (prior session and this morning's overnight), solid = today's cash session. In a drill it grows bar by bar as you advance — it never shows a print you have not reached. Outlined row = POC, tinted band = value area, right-edge ticks = HVN/LVN. A "hole" note means the tape has no prints in that window (the capture runs 02:50–15:05 CT). Press v to hide or show it.

The Phase ladder (start at 0, earn your way up)

  • 0 · Watch — nothing asked. Learn the tape's rhythm: bars now build in front of you as their trades land, and how fast they build IS the information.
  • 1 · Mechanics — factual questions about the bar that just closed (who won it, faster or slower, where the POC moved). Seeing before judging.
  • 2 · Perception — call the bar while it's still filling. Reflexes, graded.
  • 3 · Judgment — armed levels, reject/accept calls. The compass below only matters from here on.

What to look FOR (the force-and-effect compass — phase 3 material)

  • Force with effect — heavy one-sided color and price moving that way: a healthy move, expect continuation.
  • Force without effect — heavy aggressive volume but price stops: someone big is absorbing. Watch for reversal.
  • Effect without force — price makes a new high/low but the session Δ readout doesn't follow: a hollow move.
  • Footer of each column = the bar's scoreboard: the cell is shaded in the winner's color (blue buyers / red sellers); the three lines are the bar's total delta (magnitude), its duration in seconds, and its place in the cash session (1 = the first bar at or after 08:30 CT; a negative number is a pre-open bar counting down to the open — those columns are dimmed); the fill height draws that same duration on a fixed scale (full = 120s, constant across days; hover for the closing clock time). The Session Δ readout is the running sum over the cash session only (pre-open bars are not in it) — its trend matters, not its level. The Open / AM / Day chips are cash-session numbers too: Open is the first print at or after 08:30 CT, AM is 08:30–11:00, Day Hi/Lo is 08:30 to the close.

How to run a rep

  1. Or start from the Ladder dropdown: scenarios in concentration order (U0 tempo → U1 frames → S2 → S4 → S5 → S3 → S1/S6). Picking one arms its level and drops you on its fixed reference instance.
  2. Pick a level from the Level ▾ menu (the session's own levels first, then M: levels from Mancini's letter). You'll be dropped ~10 bars before price first visited it.
  3. Press Space and watch the approach: is the aggression toward the level being rewarded (bars keep moving) or eaten (heavy color, no progress)?
  4. When price gets within a point, the replay pauses and asks: reject or accept. Answer, watch the tape decide, and your running score builds below.
  5. Use the visit buttons above the chart to replay other approaches to the same level.
