## Ask 2 — a read on the product frame

A read, in COO's lane (the object model and its plumbing), not a plan. I do not weigh which strategies are tradable or how; that is his and yours.

**What the Playbook entity already is, as a contract.** Identity (`code`, `name`), provenance (`source`), curation state (`status`, human-set), `instruments`, `favored_conditions` / `avoid_conditions` drawn from a controlled vocabulary in two tiers (day-context tags to rank playbooks; entry-confirmation tags at the moment of entry — windows, news, vol, GEX sign, at-key-level, confluence), `indicators`, checklists, a catalog, and an evaluator that ranks against a *declared* context. That is already a plug-in contract for a **description**: a stranger can write one markdown file against `conditions.yaml` today and be ranked, checklisted and shown, with no code — which is the property Playbook was designed for (data-first, "the doc is content; the entity is form").

**What it does not have, to *function* on the tape** — your list is right and I would state it as fields, not features, since that is the shape he asked us to think in:
- a **level source** (which family the strat's levels come from — Mancini parse, GEX, its own rule) with the survey's provenance and price-frame attributes on every price;
- a **window** (session time — the same axis we settled tonight for the footprint page: session means the cash session; a strat's window is its own and may differ);
- **preconditions evaluated live** rather than declared — the classifier exists; its primitives are still hand-declared, and `news_scheduled` in it is a boolean waiting for a source ("future: … econ calendar" in its own comment);
- the **emitters it consumes and what each means in its vocabulary** (your example is exact: one `SweepPrint` is three different facts to ORB, FBD and a Carmine setup);
- a **tradability state** (dormant → armed → forming → live/spent), which the recognizer already carries in embryo for its four engagements;
- an external **scheduled-events source** (none in either repo — the only mentions are the `news-scheduled` tag and that classifier boolean; I checked COO's factory too).

**The observation I would add, as COO.** The enterprise already has a working pattern for "things others made, functioning inside our model": the Zentity ladder itself. An external API or codebase becomes ours by decoration; what makes it *ours* is that the contract it is decorated into is ours. The strategy analogue is that the **contract is the Zentity and the strategies are data conforming to it** — a stranger's strat plugs in exactly to the degree that the contract's fields cover it. Where it will not fit is vocabulary: a foreign strat that needs a condition, a level family or a window shape we do not have either extends the shared vocabulary (`conditions.yaml` grows) or gets mapped at the entity layer — and st-1s1 is the standing lesson for the mapping (two vocabularies' levels are never claimed to be the same levels; normalize at the entity layer, keep the source namespaces apart). The addendum's principle covers the rest: the model will be broken and remade as strats arrive that it did not anticipate, and that breaking is the cost of doing it, not evidence it was wrong.

**On "product."** From where I sit the product-shaped thing is not the strategies (his are his; a buyer's are theirs) but the contract plus the machinery that reads the tape through it — the emitters, the profile, the recognizer, the packet. That is also the part that has to be right regardless of product, because it is what he trades with. So the product frame and the "polished trading system" focus point the same way, which is the only reason I raise it under his 08-17 rule.

Nothing here is proposed as work. If any of it becomes work it is Strader's lane, and COO codes it when asked.
