# ES 05:20 CT bar, 2026-09-08 — what the tape shows

Steve asked what we can uncover about the single-candle volume spike at 05:20 CT.
The bar is in the corpus: since 2026-08-18 the collectors run the whole Globex day,
so `data/corpus/2026-09-08/` holds every ES trade and every top-of-book change
from midnight on. Everything below is measured from that file. All times are
Central. [st-l1lx]

## The read

The whole move happened in **four seconds**. Between 05:20:14 and 05:20:18 ES went
from 7706.25 to 7718.25, twelve points, on about 3,200 contracts. The book that
was hit was the normal European-hours book: 10 to 20 contracts at the touch,
one-tick spread. Buyers lifted through it faster than it could refill; several
times the bid side was down to 1 or 2 contracts while the ask stayed 10 to 15
deep, which is what a market that is being chased upward looks like.

The biggest single buy print was 157 contracts at 7710.75 at 05:20:15.68. The
biggest print of the whole minute was on the other side: **258 contracts sold at
7715.25 at 05:20:22.42**, four seconds after the high, and that second alone ran
228 contracts net sold. From there price walked back to 7711 by 05:20:41 and the
minute closed at 7711.25, giving back seven of the twelve points inside the same
bar.

For the minute as a whole: 6,732 contracts traded and net delta was only +132.
The buying and the selling were almost exactly matched, so nothing was left
behind as a position; it was a burst and its unwind. The volume-at-price ladder
says the same thing. The heaviest prices in the bar are 7715.25 and 7715.50
(433 and 410 contracts, both sold roughly 3 to 1), which is where the run was
absorbed, not where it started.

By 05:45 ES was back at 7702, below where the bar opened, and by 06:15 it was
at 7694 (your screenshot). The 7718.25 high has stood as the session high since.

## Was there news?

Not that I can find. 05:20 CT is 11:20 London, 12:20 Frankfurt; no scheduled
release sits there. The morning's live blogs (TheStreet, CNBC, Bloomberg) talk
about Houthi strikes on Saudi energy sites, Brent near $100, and Canada tariffs,
none pinned to that minute. I did not find a headline for it. The shape, a
straight-line lift into a thin book that is sold back within seconds, is what a
large market order or a short squeeze-and-fade looks like, but the tape cannot
say which. Treat the catalyst as unknown.

## One thing about the instrument

Two copies of the collector were writing today's file at the same time, so the
raw file has every trade from 03:07 on written twice, some out of order. All the
numbers above were taken after removing the duplicates by exchange sequence
number, so they are clean. The cause and the fix are on the bead **Evening Unit
Double-Writer (st-sode)**; the footprint feed has been down on the same fault
since it hit the first out-of-order row.
