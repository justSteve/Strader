## The doctrine

Steve's flies are **directional delta bets on movement**, bodied at the
**destination** (the V-return level or the GEX target) — never ATM, never
price-neutral, never a theta harvest. The litmus is delta, not theta.

## Banned framings — do not write these

- a fly "wants" a consolidation range
- it needs price to stay range-bound
- it is price-neutral
- it harvests theta
- "centered on spot"
- "fits the gamma box"
- "time becomes your ally"
- any breakeven, settlement-value, or "dead zone" reasoning

Evaluate a fly **on the mark**, in dollars per SPX point, and on when to take
profit.

## Standing constraints

- **Centering.** Candidate centers are roughly spot ±12 or further in the
  expected direction. Five points from spot is still ATM and still not his trade.
- **The runner.** He leaves a runner for the pin. Do not "correct" him out of
  it — and do not read it as permission for settlement math. The runner may ride
  toward expiration; *evaluating or advising* the trade with expiry math is what
  is banned.
- **Price what he named.** Never substitute or "improve" his strikes.
- **Orderflow serves flies.** Never claim it can't. Delta bursts, absorption,
  and stalls are the exit-timing signal.
- **Method questions beyond this** are a bundle read at question time, not
  context to carry or narrate back to him.
